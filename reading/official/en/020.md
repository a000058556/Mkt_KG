UGUARD DefenseTrust New Generation Threat Intelligence Inline Detects and Blocks Cybersecurity Attacks
Cybersecurity Service Company S-RM disclosed that ransomware Akira successfully broke into a business’ network through its Linux-based webcam clients that lack any end-point detection and response (EDR) protection. This event also reveals that in the IoT era, any connected client can be the vulnerable endpoint to a business network—especially with a diverse type of connected devices available on the market that may have compatibility issues with single EDR system, further posing a huge challenge to enterprise cybersecurity protection (in like automation application scenarios).
 UGUARD NETWORKS rolled out DefenseTrust New Generation Threat Intelligence solution that features self-established Defense Cloud for auto-collecting and compiling threat intelligence lists to perform real-time updates and imports into DefenseTrust system for proactively detecting and blocking malicious connection attacks.  
Deployed in inline mode, UGUARD DefenseTrust can help enterprises seamlessly monitor and inspect packets/traffic flow. Developed on new open-source non-SQL database structure, DefenseTrust’s comparison speed of threat intelligence lists (capable of more than 1 million records at a time) is further facilitated to thoroughly block any potential malicious connection and prevent any resultingly bandwidth waste. 
Unlike next-generation firewall that requires manual updates of threat intelligence lists, UGUARD DefenseTrust supports automatic and real-time updates and identification of effective threat intelligence lists through 13-type threat intelligence (about 1 million records) from UGUARD Defense Cloud and through other threat intelligence (about 6 million records) integrated from open-source databases or third-party organizations (e.g. X-Force) globally. With such comprehensive and accurate threat intelligence databases, DefenseTrust can effectively block more than 80% known malicious websites (such as phishing sites/malware downloads/botnets/C&C connections) and thus realize Zero-Day protection for enterprise networks.

Featuring simple and intuitive X-View centralized management reporting function, UGUARD DefenseTrust is designed for enterprise IT administrators to easily lookup and monitor status/blockage reports for both DefenseTrust and the operating business network based on specified time periods (in day/week/month/year) and user/activity group (such as service/website type). DefenseTrust X-View also supports file exports in PDF/CSV/HTML formats for easy online/offline info. lookup and sharing across different end devices anytime anywhere.
