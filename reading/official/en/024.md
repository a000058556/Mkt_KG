Taipei City, Taiwan (May 7, 2025)—For the coming annual cybersecurity event COMPUTEX 2025 this May 20 to May23, UGUARD NETWORKS will exhibit its DefenseTrust New Generation Threat Intelligence Solution, X-View Centralized Management Reporting, and AI Assistant at Booth L0329, 4th Floor, Hall 1 at Taipei Nangang Exhibition Center. This year, we will again partner with our global strategic partner AAEON Technology, a leading provider of embedded system and IoT solutions, to co-display our cybersecurity protection solutions.
UGUARD DefenseTrust can work perfectly with AAEON’s FWS-7541 Rackmount Network Appliance for delivering Zero-Day protection.  On site, we will also display AAEON’s flagship AI Inference Server, the MAXER-2100 that is capable of AI analysis on abnormal behaviors for enhancing internal network monitoring and proactive cybersecurity protection.
UGUARD DefenseTrust with AAEON FWS-7541 for Perfect Integration 
In the wave of global digitalization transformation, business enterprises are in an urgent need of high-performance threat intelligence protection solution. In regard to this trend, at COMPUTEX 2025, UGUARD NETWORKS will display its DefenseTrust new generation threat intelligence solution on AAEON’s FWS-7541 Rackmount Network Appliance to flex muscles in not only cybersecurity protection but also software-hardware integration capability that focuses on high compatibility and operation performance. 
AAEON’s FWS-7541 Rackmount Network Appliance comes with Intel® Xeon® D-1700 processor for low power consumption and high system reliability to well accommodate UGUARD DefenseTrust for delivering Zero-Day protection. UGUARD DefenseTrust is designed to proactively detect and block any potential malicious attacks through effective threat intelligence lists (up to million records) that are actively collected and compiled by UGUARD Defense Cloud’s hunting system or auto- integrated and updated from open-source or third-party threat intelligence databases globally. Such DefenseTrust on FWS-7541 integration is also in line with UGUARD NETWORKS longtime market strategy, and that is UGUARD cybersecurity solutions are for business enterprises to fast and flexibly deploy network protection.
UGUARD NETWORKS and AAEON Technology in Collaboration on Global Network Security Development
UGUARD NETWORKS Chief Executive Officer Jason Yen said, “With highly digitalized business environment, global cybersecurity safety is faced with increasing challenges that resultingly impact business organizations worldwide with continuously evolving complexity and frequency in cybersecurity attacks. In regard to such challenging cybersecurity environment, UGUARD NETWORKS and AAEON Technology again partnered up to roll out a global threat intelligence cybersecurity solution that is safe, reliable, real-time, and high-performance. Such collaboration not only strengthens website protection capability but also sets a solid foundation for ensuring data privacy and enhancing overall network resilience.”
AAEON Technology Chief Executive Officer Howard Lin said, “Our partnership with UGUARD NETWORKS shows that we are dedicated to developing and providing a first-class cybersecurity platform to suit the diverse needs of enterprises. UGUARD NETWORKS demonstrates a deep understanding of the complexity involved in developing cybersecurity solutions that is in line with AAEON’s agile approach to addressing customer needs.”
UGUARD NETWORKS All Series Solutions 
During COMPUTEX 2025, UGUARD NETWORKS will display six series solutions—Traffic Shaper, DefenseTrust, AgileLink, SD-WAN, along with X-View Centralized Management Reporting and AI Assistant—for applications/services identification on internal network(s), internal network(s) protection, Internet traffic distribution, and global cross-site Internet connection services throughout the entire business network environment. Inbuilt with X-View and AI Assistant, every UGUARD solution also comes with a simple and intuitive UI management portal that reflects UGUARD’s user-oriented design mindset. UGUARD series solutions can perfectly fit with business enterprises needs all the way from internal network(s) management, threat protection, to quality Internet connection services, and are thus suitable for single or multiple locations deployment. Further with X-View reporting system and AI Assistant, business enterprises are empowered with diverse info. charts on network status for real-time decision making and can fast set up or look up required function info. with the help of offline AI query & response function, thus thoroughly enhancing information security and operating performance of business networks.

UGUARD Solution Features at COMPUTEX 2025
I. DefenseTrust New Generation Threat Intelligence
In the face of global DNS attacks, DefenseTrust can proactively and effectively protect business networks through its real-time threat intelligence lists from UGUARD Defense Cloud and open-source/third-party databases.
Defense Cloud—collects and updates global threat intelligence lists (up to 1 million effective records) on a daily/minutely basis through deception technology and honeypot mechanism of its hunting system.
Global Threat Intelligence Integration—compiles threat intelligence lists from open-source or third-party threat intelligence databases for accurately blocking malicious IP threats and delivering Zero-Day protection.

In regard to an emerging need of business network establishment across multi- sites and regions, UGUARD AgileLink can assist business enterprises to safely integrate multiple WAN links and create virtual private networks for offices in different locations, further ensuring quality and reliable traffic transfer speed.
GTC DNS and DNSSEC security mechanism──help enterprises realize safe, reliable, and uninterrupted DNS lookup and WAN connections.
VPN and Warptunnel functions──create encrypted networks within your business networks for ensuring secure users connection and data privacy and security.

III. X-View Centralized Management Reporting
Offers integrated graphs and info. charts with diverse analytic statistics for IT administrators to easily monitor and manage their business networks and operating UGUARD series solutions (AgileLink, DefenseTrust, Traffic Shaper, and SD-WAN).
