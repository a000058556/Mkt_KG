COMPUTEX 2026 will take place from June 2 to June 5, where UGUARD NETWORKS will exhibit at Booth M0427a, 4F, Hall 1 of Taipei Nangang Exhibition Center. Together with Axiomtek, UGUARD NETWORKS will further strengthen integrated solution deployment and provide enterprises with a solid foundation for network connectivity and cybersecurity protection.
Under the theme “From Connectivity, Traffic to Threat Intelligence,” UGUARD NETWORKS will showcase its comprehensive capabilities in enterprise networking and cybersecurity integration:

AgileLink WAN and LEO Satellite Integrated Architecture
Combining multi-WAN management with Low Earth Orbit (LEO) satellite communication technologies, AgileLink delivers a highly available and resilient enterprise network infrastructure, ensuring critical business operations remain continuously connected in any environment.

DefenseTrust & DefenseCloud Threat Intelligence Real-Time Defense Architecture
Powered by a global threat hunting system with threat intelligence synchronized and updated every 3 minutes, combined with AI-driven cybersecurity reporting and analytics, the platform proactively identifies malicious connections and abnormal traffic patterns, enabling real-time threat blocking before attacks enter the enterprise network.

AgileCloud AI-Driven Cloud Monitoring and Intelligent Alerting
An AI-powered cloud management platform that provides cross-site visualized cloud reporting, hardware anomaly detection, and intelligent alert notifications, helping IT teams evolve from passive operations management to proactive decision-making.

AgileShaper Next-Generation Traffic Governance Platform (Coming Soon)
The upcoming AgileShaper integrates Application Visibility, Traffic Shaping, SSL Proxy, NAC Access Control, and intelligent bandwidth orchestration to accurately identify critical applications and prioritize network resources. It ensures stable performance for ERP, video conferencing, cloud SaaS, and mission-critical business systems — establishing a new enterprise networking standard centered on “Business-First” traffic management

	
One of the highlights of this year’s exhibition is our collaboration with industrial computing leader Axiomtek, jointly showcasing innovative networking and cybersecurity solutions.

Together, UGUARD NETWORKS and Axiomtek will demonstrate integrated networking and security architectures spanning from the edge to the cloud. By combining UGUARD NETWORKS’ software technologies with Axiomtek’s high-performance and highly reliable hardware platforms, both companies help enterprises rapidly build scalable, high-performance, and secure network infrastructures — accelerating digital transformation and cybersecurity modernization.
