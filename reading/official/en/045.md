Establishing a One-Stop Cybersecurity Service Model through Strategic Alliance

【Taipei News, March 26, 2026】 UGUARD NETWORKS, a Singapore-based cybersecurity company, today announced the official signing of a distribution partnership with MetaAge (6112), a leading IT intelligent solutions provider. By combining their respective strengths in network security technologies and local market services, both parties aim to further expand and strengthen Taiwan’s cybersecurity market.

  
This collaboration marks a strategic alliance in which MetaAge becomes a key distribution partner of UGUARD NETWORKS. UGUARD will also officially join MetaAge’s “One-Stop Cybersecurity Service” ecosystem, working together to deliver a more comprehensive and integrated cybersecurity architecture for enterprise customers.

  
Building a One-Stop Cybersecurity Service Framework to Strengthen Enterprise Defense

Amid accelerating digital transformation and hybrid work trends, enterprises are facing increasingly complex cybersecurity threats. MetaAge integrates multiple cybersecurity brands and technologies to deliver a “one-stop cybersecurity service,” covering the entire IT security lifecycle—from prevention and detection to response—helping enterprises build a comprehensive and resilient defense framework.

  
Four Core Cybersecurity Domains by UGUARD NETWORKS

UGUARD NETWORKS integrates technologies across four key cybersecurity domains: Threat Intelligence, Cloud Platform, AI Network Analytics, and Perimeter Security,forming a complete enterprise-grade cybersecurity defense system. This enables organizations to respond to increasingly sophisticated threats with enhanced real-time visibility and proactive defense capabilities.

  
UGUARD’s next-generation cybersecurity architecture is built upon:
Threat Intelligence + Cloud Platform + WAN Network Security + AI Analytics,delivering a forward-looking and adaptive security framework.

  
Driving Next-Generation One-Stop Cybersecurity Services with MetaAge

With the rapid evolution of global cybersecurity threats and the acceleration of digitalization and cloud adoption, building a highly resilient and real-time defense architecture has become a critical foundation for enterprise operations.

UGUARD NETWORKS has long focused on WAN technologies and threat intelligence, continuously investing in innovation in network resilience and cybersecurity defense. Meanwhile,MetaAge brings strong local expertise with a well-established distribution network and professional cybersecurity services in Taiwan.

  
Executive Perspectives

Jason Yen, CEO of UGUARD NETWORKS, stated:
“As cybersecurity threats continue to evolve toward real-time and globalized attack patterns, traditional hardware-based defense architectures are no longer sufficient. UGUARD NETWORKS is committed to building a forward-looking cybersecurity framework by integrating the DefenseCloud global threat intelligence platform and AgileCloud management platform, combined with AI-driven WAN traffic analytics and real-time alerting capabilities. This enables enterprises to establish a Proactive Cyber Defense Architecture, allowing them to anticipate threats before they occur and respond rapidly.”

“This partnership with MetaAge not only represents a key milestone in UGUARD’s market expansion, but also symbolizes our joint commitment to advancing a One-Stop Cybersecurity Service Model, delivering more comprehensive and real-time protection for enterprise customers.”

  
Shih Chien-Cheng, General Manager of MetaAge, commented:
“MetaAge has long been deeply rooted in Taiwan’s networking and cybersecurity service market. As enterprises face increasingly stringent security and compliance requirements, there is a growing demand for more immediate and integrated protection solutions. UGUARD NETWORKS’ technological strengths and threat intelligence integration capabilities will enable us to deliver more comprehensive cybersecurity architectures to our customers.”
