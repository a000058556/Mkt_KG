TrafficShaper uses Layer 7 visibility to simplify network management.
When an enterprise network suddenly slows down, the first reaction is often, "Is there not enough bandwidth?"
However, the real cause of network congestion may not be insufficient bandwidth. Certain applications, users, or services may be consuming excessive network resources. For example, video streaming, cloud services, file transfers, SaaS applications, video conferencing, and even background automatic updates can unknowingly affect critical business services such as ERP, VPN, and servers.
If IT teams can only see "how much total bandwidth is being used" but cannot identify who is using it, which applications are consuming it, or where the traffic is going, simply adding more bandwidth may only provide a temporary solution.

The First Step in Network Management Is Not Limiting Bandwidth, but Understanding Traffic
TrafficShaper uses Layer 7 Application Identification to help IT teams gain clear visibility into application and traffic usage across enterprise networks, enabling them to quickly identify:
✓ Which applications consume the most bandwidth
✓ Which users or devices consume the most network resources
✓ Which domains and services are the main sources of network traffic
✓ Whether there are abnormal trends in inbound and outbound bandwidth usage
✓ Whether non-essential traffic is affecting critical business services
Helping IT teams move beyond the vague complaint of "the network is slow" to quickly identify the root cause.

Are You Experiencing These Network Challenges?
1. Upgraded your bandwidth, but the network is still slow?

2. Are ERP, VPN, and video conferencing frequently affected by other traffic?

3. Does IT still need to troubleshoot network issues one by one?
Without traffic visibility, identifying the root cause can be time-consuming.
4. Not sure how much bandwidth SaaS, video streaming, and cloud services consume?

5. Have traffic data but struggle to turn it into management policies?

From Visibility to Smarter Network Management
TrafficShaper goes beyond traffic monitoring, enabling enterprises to establish traffic management policies based on application and business requirements:
·      Critical Services: ERP, VPN, CRM, and video conferencing — prioritize bandwidth for business-critical applications.
·      General Office Applications: Web, Email, Office 365, and Google Workspace — allocate bandwidth according to business needs.
·      Non-Critical Applications: YouTube, large file downloads, and video streaming — limit bandwidth or lower traffic priority.
TrafficShaper ensures that limited network resources are prioritized for the enterprise services that matter most.
