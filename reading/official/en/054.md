DefenseCloud transforms millions of real-time cyber attack events into an interactive global threat map, providing organizations with instant visibility into attack origins, threat hotspots, and emerging cyber trends. With threat intelligence covering 224 countries and regions and continuously updated through a global honeypot network, organizations can quickly identify high-risk attack sources and proactively strengthen their security posture before threats impact their business.
 Through an intuitive visualization dashboard and real-time analytics, security teams gain clear insights into the distribution and scale of cyber threats, enabling them to implement more effective security strategies such as traffic control, malicious connection blocking, and automated threat response.
Real-Time On-Premises Threat Protection
DefenseTrust provides a comprehensive IP and domain threat intelligence database to help organizations automatically block known malicious threats, including phishing websites, malware downloads, botnets, and command-and-control (C&C) communications.
With Inline deployment, DefenseTrust delivers high-speed threat inspection with minimal network latency while continuously synchronizing the latest threat intelligence. Fully automated threat updates significantly reduce administrative workload and enable organizations to respond to emerging threats more efficiently. Built-in hardware bypass and software bypass mechanisms ensure continuous network availability, minimizing production downtime caused by device or power failures.
