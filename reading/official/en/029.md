【Taipei, June 26, 2025】To strengthen local channel collaboration and cybersecurity service coverage in Taiwan, network security provider UGUARD NETWORKS will officially enter into a strategic partnership with Taiwan-based distributor PetaCom Technology Inc. (PETACOM). The two companies will host a signing ceremony and product update event on July 3, 2025, aimed at jointly promoting enterprise-grade cybersecurity solutions across Taiwan.

The event will be held at JR East Hotel Taipei, where PETACOM’s distribution partners are invited to witness the signing and explore technical highlights and live demonstrations of UGUARD’s key solutions — DefenseTrust, AgileLink, and X-View.

UGUARD NETWORKS On-Site Solution Highlights
The event will feature live demos of several UGUARD intelligent cybersecurity solutions, including:
AgileLink: A next-generation WAN management solution with Global Traffic Collaboration DNS and DNSSEC protection to reinforce enterprise DNS architecture.
DefenseTrust: An advanced threat intelligence solution that automatically and precisely integrates global threat data, empowering enterprises with zero-day attack defense capabilities.
UGUARD X-View: An integrated management reporting platform that offers comprehensive views and multidimensional analysis for smarter, centralized network operations and security management.
UGUARD AI Threat Intelligence Report: Using AI analysis of threat intelligence hits via DefenseTrust, this tool provides IT teams with user-friendly reports that accelerate threat mitigation.
