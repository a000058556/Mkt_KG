With the rapid adoption of AI applications, SaaS cloud services, and remote work, enterprise network traffic has grown significantly. From ChatGPT, Microsoft 365, Zoom, and Google Workspace to ERP systems and various cloud services, daily network environments are becoming increasingly complex.
However, as these applications consume more bandwidth, organizations often face common challenges such as lagging video conferences, slow ERP and critical systems, and bandwidth being occupied by non-essential applications.
Without proper traffic visibility and management, simply increasing bandwidth may not effectively resolve network performance issues.

Enterprise Network Traffic Management Challenges			

In the era of AI and SaaS adoption, IT teams commonly face the following challenges:
 Rapid growth of AI and cloud traffic:
Applications like ChatGPT, AI tools, and SaaS services consume significant bandwidth, leading to network congestion.
 Lack of prioritization for critical applications:
Business-critical systems such as ERP and CRM are often affected by other traffic and cannot be prioritized effectively.
 Limited traffic visibility and analytics:
IT teams struggle to identify which applications are consuming bandwidth.
 Difficulty in identifying network performance issues:
Users only experience “slow networks,” while the root cause remains unclear.
Without a well-defined traffic management strategy, enterprises will find it difficult to resolve network issues caused by AI and cloud applications.
