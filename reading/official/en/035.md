As SaaS, cloud services, video conferencing, and AI applications become part of daily enterprise operations, network traffic is no longer just about “how fast the internet is.” Instead, it is about whether critical services are properly prioritized, user behavior is fully visible, and anomalies can be detected and traced in real time. Relying solely on traditional firewalls or bandwidth upgrades is no longer sufficient to handle the complexity of modern application traffic and the growing demands of network management.

UGUARD Traffic Shaper is purpose-built for modern enterprises. With Layer 7 application identification and intelligent traffic management, it enables IT teams to clearly understand who is using which applications and services, and at what time.

By applying precise bandwidth allocation and control based on users, groups, applications, and website categories, UGUARD Traffic Shaper ensures stable performance for mission-critical business operations, cloud systems, and video conferencing services.

Traffic Shaper features an intuitive, visualized dashboard that transforms complex network traffic data into real-time charts, enabling IT administrators to quickly gain a clear overview of overall network usage.

Through Layer 7 QoS application-layer traffic identification, the system clearly presents real-time traffic dynamics across different application services, including upload/download trends, top application rankings, and traffic distribution ratios, empowering IT teams to make more accurate and informed management decisions.

The interface consolidates key metrics such as device status, CPU and memory utilization, and concurrent connection counts, while providing real-time visibility into Top Domains and service traffic distribution. This allows administrators to quickly identify high-traffic sources and potential performance bottlenecks.

When combined with user identity recognition and authentication mechanisms, Traffic Shaper enables more effective bandwidth control and traffic optimization, ensuring priority for critical applications while significantly reducing the operational and response burden on IT teams.

In the cloud- and AI-driven era, Traffic Shaper is more than just a traffic management solution—it is a critical tool that helps enterprises enhance network experience, strengthen visibility, and improve operational efficiency.
