Today’s Enterprise Pain Points

Traditional security defenses rely heavily on signatures and known samples. However, modern cyberattacks are increasingly stealthy, real-time, and automated. Enterprises are now facing the following challenges:
Rapidly evolving threats, creating a time gap in signature updates
Inefficient manual blacklisting on firewalls, leading to device overload and ineffective protection
End-of-life (EOL) devices (such as IXIA) lacking updates and ongoing maintenance
Diverse global attack vectors, including malicious IPs, botnets, phishing websites, and APT attacks
Increasingly stringent cybersecurity compliance requirements (ISO 27001:2022)
Attacks no longer follow predictable patterns
Cross-border and cross-cloud threat sources
Rapid mutation and variation of malware

Without a global perspective and real-time intelligence analysis capabilities, enterprises will struggle to identify risks at the earliest stage.
