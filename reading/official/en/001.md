We provide you with a better network solution:
AgileLink New Generation WAN Management (NGWM)
AgileLink offers an integrated solution for both virtual and physical lines, helping administrators consolidate multiple connections and establish virtual bandwidth between different physical offices, improving the speed, stability, and quality of critical traffic transmission. When a line failure occurs, AgileLink automatically selects a higher-quality line for data transmission, ensuring that even if users are on a VoIP call at the moment of failover, there is no interruption or delay. AgileLink features line status detection — when a line anomaly is detected, it automatically redirects the affected service traffic to a healthy line, preventing service disruptions from impacting business operations. Through the integration of DNS resolution and line status detection, AgileLink effectively maintains continuous enterprise service availability.
