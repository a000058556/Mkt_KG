UGUARD AgileLink Next-Generation WAN Management – Enabling Enterprises to Build High-Speed, Stable, and Secure Network Services

Singapore (September 3, 2025 )– UGUARD NETWORKS, a global leader in cybersecurity and communication innovation, announced that on the occasion of the first anniversary of its Singapore headquarters, the company has successfully completed the development and integration of STARLINK Low Earth Orbit (LEO) satellite technology into its AgileLink product line. Starting today and running until December 31, 2026.

UGUARD NETWORKS will launch the “Free Enterprise Health and Check Service for Existing Customers in Taiwan”, providing professional assessments and consulting services to help enterprises gain a comprehensive understanding of their current network environment. This initiative reflects the company’s dual commitment to technological innovation and corporate social responsibility, supporting enterprises in building networks that are faster, more stable, and more secure.

In August 2025, UGUARD NETWORKS dispatched its entire R&D division to Tokyo, Japan, to focus on LEO satellite integration. By combining this breakthrough with its strengths in cybersecurity, the company successfully advanced the integration of STARLINK satellite technology. This achievement enables enterprises, healthcare institutions, and public organizations in Taiwan and across the Asia-Pacific region to overcome the limitations of traditional network infrastructure and benefit from high-speed, reliable, and secure connectivity.

As enterprises accelerate digital transformation and cloud adoption, WAN management and cybersecurity challenges grow increasingly complex. UGUARD NETWORKS’ Singapore headquarters will continue to develop around its core pillars of LEO satellite integration and cybersecurity, expanding global market reach while creating long-term value for customers and society through innovation. The health-check program will leverage the AgileLink New Generation WAN Management platform to help enterprises review their current architecture and maintain network performance amid rising connectivity demands and cyber threats.

Why UGUARD NETWORKS?
Since its founding, UGUARD NETWORKS has focused on independent R&D and professional reliability. Unlike vendors that simply provide hardware, UGUARD emphasizes continuous customer care. This free enterprise health-check program is not only a gesture of appreciation for long-term customers, but also a tangible action that delivers on the company’s promise: “Supporting our customers’ growth and ensuring sustainable, reliable networks.”

Jason Yen, CEO of UGUARD NETWORKS, commented
“In August 2025, during our R&D efforts in Tokyo, we not only completed the integration of STARLINK LEO satellite technology but also launched the Taiwan Free Enterprise Health-Check Service. This demonstrates that UGUARD NETWORKS is not only at the forefront of technological innovation but also fully committed to our mission of ‘Safeguarding Connectivity.’ Looking ahead, we will continue to strengthen our presence in Singapore and across the Asia-Pacific region, advancing the convergence of cybersecurity and communication to deliver superior network quality for our global customers.”
