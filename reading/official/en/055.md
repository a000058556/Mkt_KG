AI is reshaping cyberattacks. DefenseTrust delivers real-time protection against today's evolving threats.
In recent years, the rapid adoption of AI technologies has not only enhanced business productivity but also empowered cybercriminals with more sophisticated attack capabilities. Today's cyberattacks are no longer limited to large-scale malware campaigns or Distributed Denial-of-Service (DDoS) attacks. Instead, attackers leverage AI, automation, and globally distributed malicious infrastructures to continuously change attack sources, conceal their activities, and launch more targeted, stealthy attacks.
Cybercriminals frequently rotate malicious IP addresses and domains, and even abuse legitimate cloud services as stepping stones to build agile attack infrastructures that can be replaced within minutes. Many malicious connections appear almost identical to legitimate network traffic, making them extremely difficult to detect. By the time suspicious activity is discovered, sensitive data may have already been compromised, or ransomware may have already infiltrated the organization's network.
