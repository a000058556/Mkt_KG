[Taipei News, May 8, 2026] COMPUTEX 2026 will take place from June 2 to June 5, and UGUARD NETWORKS will exhibit at Taipei Nangang Exhibition Center Hall 1, 4F (Booth M0427a). UGUARD NETWORKS will also partner with Axiomtek to further strengthen integrated solution deployment and provide enterprises with solid network and cybersecurity protection.

At this year’s exhibition, UGUARD NETWORKS will showcase its core solutions, including AgileLink, DefenseTrust, Traffic Shaper, AgileCloud, DefenseCloud, and the upcoming new product AgileShaper. Covering WAN management, threat defense, traffic management, and AI-powered cloud management platforms, these solutions integrate both software and hardware technologies to help enterprises maintain stable connectivity, achieve traffic visibility, and enable real-time cybersecurity protection — all within a unified platform architecture.

With high-performance scalability and integrated network security capabilities, UGUARD NETWORKS is committed to delivering a comprehensive next-generation networking and cybersecurity solution for enterprises.
UGUARD NETWORKS’ exhibition theme, “From Connectivity and Traffic to Threat Intelligence,” fully demonstrates its integrated enterprise networking and cybersecurity capabilities:

AgileLink WAN and LEO Satellite Integrated Architecture
By combining multi-WAN management with Low Earth Orbit (LEO) satellite communication technologies, AgileLink delivers a highly available and resilient enterprise network infrastructure, ensuring critical business operations remain continuously connected in any environment.

DefenseTrust & DefenseCloud Threat Intelligence Real-Time Defense Architecture
Powered by a global threat hunting system with threat intelligence synchronized and updated every 3 minutes, combined with AI-driven cybersecurity reporting and analytics, the platform proactively identifies malicious connections and abnormal traffic patterns, enabling real-time threat blocking before attacks enter the enterprise network.

AgileCloud AI-Driven Cloud Monitoring and Intelligent Alerting
An AI-powered cloud management platform that provides cross-site visualized cloud reporting, hardware anomaly detection, and intelligent alert notifications, helping IT teams evolve from passive operations management to proactive decision-making.

AgileShaper Next-Generation Traffic Governance Platform (Coming Soon)
The upcoming AgileShaper integrates Application Visibility, Traffic Shaping, SSL Proxy, NAC access control, and intelligent bandwidth orchestration to accurately identify critical applications and prioritize resource allocation. This ensures stable performance for ERP systems, video conferencing, cloud SaaS platforms, and core business applications — establishing a new “business-first” standard for enterprise network management.

Exhibition Highlight of the Year! Partnering with Industrial PC Leader Axiomtek to Showcase Innovative Networking Solutions

This year, UGUARD NETWORKS is partnering with Axiomtek to jointly showcase integrated networking and cybersecurity solutions spanning from edge to cloud. By integrating UGUARD NETWORKS’ software technologies with Axiomtek’s high-performance and highly reliable hardware platforms, the two companies help enterprises rapidly build scalable, high-performance, and highly secure network infrastructures to accelerate digital transformation and cybersecurity modernization.
At the exhibition, Axiomtek will showcase multiple networking and embedded platforms designed to support enterprise deployment requirements across various environments:

	
High-Performance Core Network Appliance Platform
Powered by the latest-generation Intel® processor architecture, these platforms deliver powerful computing performance and flexible scalability for high-traffic and high-density computing environments. The featured NA873 (2U) platform serves as a core infrastructure platform for UGUARD WAN management and cybersecurity protection systems, supporting network virtualization, AI-powered traffic analytics, and real-time threat defense.

Industrial-Grade Edge Cybersecurity & Embedded Computing Platforms 
Combining cybersecurity gateway capabilities with fanless embedded systems, these platforms are specifically designed for smart manufacturing and critical infrastructure environments, featuring DIN-Rail support, LAN Bypass, and high operational stability. Axiomtek’s industrial-grade platforms, including the iNA110 / iNA200 / iNA300 and ICO110 / ICO130 / ICO150 / ICO340 series, are ideal for OT cybersecurity and harsh industrial edge computing deployments, strengthening threat protection, traffic management, and operational continuity.

Axiomtek provides comprehensive hardware support ranging from core network appliances to edge computing nodes, forming a tightly integrated solution with UGUARD NETWORKS’ WAN management, traffic control, and threat intelligence platforms. Together, they deliver a unified architecture covering “Connectivity, Traffic, and Threat Intelligence.”
