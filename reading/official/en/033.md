With the surge of remote work, global branch operations, and AI cloud applications, video conferencing, ERP, and cloud backups now compete for the same bandwidth — causing potential slowdowns during peak hours. Is your enterprise ready to tackle the rising challenges of network interruptions and cybersecurity threats? Below are the common pain points enterprises face in network operations:
• Submarine Cable Outage Risk: Recent frequent failures of international submarine cables around Taiwan have made cross-border connection delays and interruptions a regular occurrence.
• AI Security Challenges: Generative AI–driven attacks such as deepfakes, fake domains, and phishing emails continue to evolve, making remote access points more vulnerable.
• Cross-Border Enterprise Threats: Semiconductor and manufacturing sectors are often targeted by state-sponsored APT groups (e.g., APT41, FistBump) attempting to infiltrate branch networks and steal R&D data.
• Hybrid Work Trend: Remote employees, international teams, and external supply chains accessing internal systems have become the new normal.
UGUARD NETWORKS Solution — WarpTunnel™:
AgileLink WarpTunnel provides a high-security, high-availability VPN/cloud tunnel to ensure enterprises stay securely connected anytime, anywhere. Key Features —
1. End-to-End Encryption: Prevents eavesdropping and spoofed logins, ensuring all access is authentic and trusted.
2. Multipath Redundancy + Auto Failover: Automatically switches to a backup route during submarine cable or cross-border routing failures — maintaining uninterrupted connectivity.
3. Low-Latency Optimization: Integrates LACP and smart routing to enhance the stability of AI training, cross-border ERP, and cloud applications.
4. Visualized Management: Provides real-time dashboards and reports to help IT teams monitor remote access traffic and potential risks.
